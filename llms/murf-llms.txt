# Murf AI

> Murf Studio is a cloud-based realistic text-to-speech platform that allows users to generate lifelike AI-based voiceovers for multiple use cases.

**Platform Solutions**

- [AI Voice Generation](https://murf.ai/): Create realistic AI voices for any project using Murf's AI voice generation technology.
- [AI Text-to-Speech](https://murf.ai/text-to-speech): Convert text into natural-sounding speech using cutting-edge AI-powered technology..
- [AI Voice Cloning](https://murf.ai/voice-cloning): Create custom AI voice clones that mimic human emotions for various applications like advertising and content creation.
- [AI Voice Changer](https://murf.ai/voice-changer): Modify and transform voices in real-time with advanced AI-powered voice changing.
- [Text-to-Speech API](https://murf.ai/api): Facilitates integration of Murf's text-to-speech capabilities into applications with diverse voice options.
- [Audio to Text Conversion](https://murf.ai/audio-to-text-converter): Convert audio files into accurate, editable text with powerful AI transcription.
- [Voice to Text Conversion](https://murf.ai/speech-to-text): Transform spoken words into text with high-accuracy speech recognition.
- [Voice Over Video Creation](https://murf.ai/voice-over-video): Create engaging voiceovers for videos using AI-generated voices in various languages.
- [Online Voice Editing Tool](https://murf.ai/voice-editing): Murf offers an intuitive online platform for editing voiceovers by converting audio recordings into editable text format.
- [Online Audio Translation Tool](https://murf.ai/tools/audio-translator): Murf offers free online audio to audio translation across 10+ languages and 200+ AI voices..
